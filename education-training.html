Service 03 Education & training

Education and training that carries dialysis teams to certification

We prepare dialysis technicians, nurses, and program leaders through structured orientation, competency assessment, and BONENT-approved certification instruction.

In-person programs are delivered on site across New York, Connecticut, and New Jersey or scheduled as cohorts, and are documented so training records stand up to survey review. Certification exam preparation is also available nationwide through our online LMS.

Curriculum

Eight programs from orientation to leadership

Take a single program or build a full annual education calendar for your facility.

01

Staff orientation programs

Structured onboarding to facility policy, machine operation, infection control, and patient safety expectations.

02

Clinical in-service education

Targeted in-services on practice changes, equipment updates, and findings from your own quality data.

03

Competency assessments

Written and hands-on assessment against dialysis-specific standards, with documentation for personnel files.

04

CPR certification

Initial and renewal CPR certification scheduled around treatment hours, on site at your facility.

05

National certification exam preparation

BONENT-approved instruction preparing dialysis technicians and nurses for national certification examination — available nationwide through our online LMS.

06

Continuing education programs

Ongoing coursework that keeps certified staff current and maintains recertification requirements.

07

Regulatory compliance training

Staff education on CMS conditions for coverage, accreditation expectations, and AAMI practice requirements.

08

Leadership development

Preparation for charge nurse, clinical coordinator, and program leadership responsibilities.

Curriculum approval

Our dialysis education curriculum is approved by BONENT

Board of Nephrology Examiners Nursing and Technology — the national body whose certification your technicians and nurses sit for. Approval means the instruction we deliver is measured against that standard, not against a generic training package.

Clinical experience & partnerships

Instruction reinforced by manufacturers and the clinical setting

Training programs include specialized education delivered with dialysis equipment manufacturers, alongside clinical exposure arranged through our institutional partners.

Manufacturer education
