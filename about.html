About the firm

A Nephrology-specialized partner to dialysis providers across NY, CT & NJ

HHD Renal Consultant & Biomedical Services, Inc. provides staffing, biomedical, and education solutions to dialysis providers across the tri-state area — New York, Connecticut, and New Jersey — with BONENT-approved certification training available nationwide, backed by decades of clinical and operational experience.

Our work sits where clinical practice, equipment reliability, and regulatory expectation meet — in-center, acute, and home hemodialysis programs that cannot afford a gap in coverage, a machine out of specification, or a team unprepared for survey.

Founder

A career built on the dialysis treatment floor

Kazim R. Naqvi is a Nephrology leader with more than 27 years of dialysis experience spanning clinical operations, equipment and water treatment management, and the education of technicians and nurses.

He founded HHD Renal Consultant & Biomedical Services, Inc. to bring high-quality consulting, education, staffing, and biomedical services to the dialysis community — one standard of practice, applied whether the engagement is a single technician placement or a full program review.

The experience behind the firm is the same experience every HHD clinician is measured against before a placement is confirmed.

  • Dialysis operations & program leadership
  • National certification instruction
  • Biomedical & water treatment oversight
  • Regulatory & survey preparation

Our commitment

A rigorous selection process, applied to every placement

Biomedical engineers, dialysis nurses, technicians, and caregivers are matched carefully to each facility. Five checks stand between a candidate and your treatment floor.

  1. 01

    Background screening

    Criminal, employment, and reference screening completed before a candidate is presented.

  2. 02

    License & certification verification

    Primary-source verification of state licensure and current national certification.

  3. 03

    Competency assessment

    Written and practical assessment against dialysis-specific competency standards.

  4. 04

    Clinical skills validation

    Hands-on validation of cannulation, machine setup, water treatment, and infection control practice.

  5. 05

    Ongoing performance evaluation

    Documented review with facility leadership through the life of the assignment.
