Survey readiness

How a readiness engagement runs

  1. 01

    Gap assessment

    On-site review of policy, records, water and equipment logs, and practice against applicable standards.

  2. 02

    Written findings & plan

    Prioritized findings with a corrective action plan, owners, and target dates your leadership can act on.

  3. 03

    Remediation support

    Policy rewrites, staff education, and biomedical correction delivered by the relevant HHD division.

  4. 04

    Mock survey & sign-off

    Tracer-style walkthrough with staff interviews, then a closing report confirming readiness.
