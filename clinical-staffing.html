Service 01 Clinical staffing

Clinical staffing for in-center, acute, and home hemodialysis

We provide highly qualified, carefully vetted clinical staff for in-center, acute, and home hemodialysis programs across New York, Connecticut, and New Jersey.

Every clinician clears background screening, primary-source license verification, competency assessment, and hands-on skills validation before an assignment begins — and is reviewed with your leadership throughout it.

What we staff

Five staffing services across the care setting

Engage a single shift, a sustained contract, or a full program build. Scope is agreed with your Director of Nursing before placement.

01

In-center hemodialysis staffing

RNs, LPNs, and patient care technicians for chronic outpatient units — shift coverage, leave backfill, census growth, and new-clinic openings.

  • Charge nurse & floor RN coverage
  • Certified patient care technicians
  • Travel & contract assignments
02

Acute dialysis staffing

Hospital-based acute teams for inpatient hemodialysis, CRRT support, and ICU bedside treatment, including on-call and weekend coverage.

  • Acute-experienced dialysis RNs
  • On-call & after-hours rotation
  • Portable RO & machine competency
03

Home hemodialysis staffing

HHD nurses, technicians, caregivers, and patient support staff for home programs — training, follow-up, and continuity of care between visits.

  • HHD training nurses
  • Home program technicians
  • Patient support & follow-up staff
04

Certified HHD caregivers

Caregivers trained and certified specifically for home hemodialysis, placed with patients whose treatment depends on a competent partner at the machine.

  • HHD-specific caregiver certification
  • Skills validated before placement
  • Ongoing competency review
05

Transportation & logistics support

Coordination of patient transportation and supply logistics for home hemodialysis programs, so treatment schedules hold without administrative strain on your staff.

  • Patient transportation coordination
  • Home supply & delivery scheduling
  • Treatment-schedule continuity
